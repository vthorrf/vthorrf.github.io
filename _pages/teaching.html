Teaching approach

I try to connect formal methods to the scientific decisions they support. In quantitative courses, the emphasis is not only on how to fit a model, but also on what assumptions the model makes, what evidence can challenge those assumptions, and how results should be communicated. In research and writing courses, I use the same principle: methods are most useful when they sharpen the underlying scientific question.
